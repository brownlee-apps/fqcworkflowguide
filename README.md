# FQ&C Workflow Guide

A guide for Bossier Parish Community College's Office of the Registrar and Human Resources to the Faculty Qualifications and Credentialing approval workflow: the emails each office receives from it, what each one asks, and what happens after each answer. The emails and pages it pictures are the workflow's own, shown with invented records.

`index.html`, `assets/`, and `shots/` are built from the guide's source, kept by the Office of Academic Affairs. Edit the source and rebuild rather than editing these files by hand.
